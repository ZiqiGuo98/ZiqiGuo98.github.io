Excited to share three new papers this year:

- **Machine Learning Inverse Design Reveals a Double Narrow-Band Absorption Approach for Effective Colored Radiative Cooling Paints**, *Nano Letters* (first author, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.nanolett.6c01941)). A generative inverse-design pipeline combining neural surrogates with multi-objective optimization nearly doubles visible reflectance (40% to 76%) compared with conventional color designs at matched color.
- **FourPhonon_GPU, a CPU-GPU heterogeneous computing scheme for phonon scattering and thermal conductivity calculations**, *Cell Reports Physical Science* (first author, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrp.2026.103491)). See [the full announcement](/News/News_17/).
- **Biodegradable Paints for Daytime Radiative Cooling**, *Nano Letters* (co-author, led by Ziqi Fang, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.nanolett.6c01376)).

I also have a co-authored *Physical Review B* paper on first-principles infrared optical properties of insulators ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/4b8p-gysh)). All papers are listed in [Publications](/publications/).
