Two papers on daytime radiative cooling paints appeared in *Nano Letters* in 2026:

- **Machine Learning Inverse Design Reveals a Double Narrow-Band Absorption Approach for Effective Colored Radiative Cooling Paints** (first author, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.nanolett.6c01941)). A generative inverse-design pipeline combining neural surrogates with multi-objective optimization nearly doubles visible reflectance (40% to 76%) compared with conventional color designs at matched color.
- **Biodegradable Paints for Daytime Radiative Cooling** (co-author), led by Ziqi Fang. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.nanolett.6c01376)
