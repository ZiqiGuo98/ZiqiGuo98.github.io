## Research awards

**Dimitris N. Chorafas Foundation Award** (2025)
: An international foundation award for outstanding doctoral research in the sciences, engineering, and medicine. Recipients come from a small set of partner research universities, and only about 20 awards are given worldwide each year. Awarded for my research in physics and AI. [Details](/News/News_14/)

**Graduate Student Gold Award, Materials Research Society** (2025)
: The highest recognition the Materials Research Society gives to graduate students, awarded at the 2025 MRS Spring Meeting & Exhibit based on research quality and significance, published record, and presentation. I also served as a session chair of Symposium SF01: *Thermal Transport in Materials*. [Details](/News/News_13/)

**Magoon Research Excellence Award, Purdue University College of Engineering** (2025)
: For outstanding research performance during the Ph.D.; limited to four students per year across the College of Engineering. [Details](/News/News_12/)

**Warren and Judy Stevenson Scholarship, Purdue School of Mechanical Engineering** (2024)
: Awarded to a small number of graduate students each year for outstanding research in applied optics. [Details](/News/News_08/)

**Outstanding Presentation Awards (×2), ASME IMECE** (2024)
: Two awards at the ASME International Mechanical Engineering Congress and Exposition, for talks in the sessions *Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Data Science for Thermal Processes, Heat Transfer and Energy Systems* and *Radiation Across Scales*. [Details](/News/News_09/)

**Best Presentation Award, ASME Summer Heat Transfer Conference** (2023)
: Awarded for the best presentation in the session at the ASME 2023 Heat Transfer Summer Conference. [Details](/News/News_02/)
